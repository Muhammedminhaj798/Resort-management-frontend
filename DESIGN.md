# Design System: Kerala (working title)

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Editorial Quarterly"**

Every page is an issue of a print travel quarterly about Kerala. The homepage is this season's issue: a cover you book from, a contents page you browse by, and features set as spreads. The system is printed, not rendered: forest-green spot ink laid down as whole spreads, uncoated ivory stock for the reading spreads, and a muted gold foil kept for a handful of marks. Hierarchy comes from scale and face, not from weight, colour badges, or containers.

Density is low and the pace is a magazine's. Sections are full-width grounds with generous vertical padding, content sits on a 12-column grid inside a 1440px measure, and photographs are framed square, captioned, and credited like plates. Motion is settled: content reveals once as it arrives and then stays at rest.

The user pinned the palette (forest greens, warm ivory, earthy neutrals, muted gold) and the pairing of serif headings with sans body. The world refuses the full-bleed photo with a floating white search pill and rows of rounded cards.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Whole-section grounds alternate between green spreads and ivory reading spreads.
- Gold is foil: four roles only.
- Brygada 1918 at regular weight carries every heading; Anek Latin carries reading and interface text, with a narrow Anek for captions.
- Square corners everywhere; depth from grounds and hairline rules, not shadows.
- Every photograph has a caption and credit; every sample has a visible "Placeholder" tag; every price has a breakdown.
- English and Malayalam are first-class; Malayalam gets its own line-heights and sizes.

## Colors

A forest-and-ivory print palette with one muted gold foil and one earth neutral, all warm and low in saturation.

### Primary
- **Canopy Ink** (canopy): the deepest green and the page's base ground, used for the cover, Field Notes, the body background, and text on ivory. Also the focus colour on ivory grounds.
- **Forest Spot Ink** (forest): the second green spread (Packages, For Hosts), the folio strip, the condensed top bar, and the mobile menu. On ivory it marks calendar selections and the Sea to the Ghats land mass.

### Secondary
- **Gold Foil** (gold): the fill of the primary button and the Sea to the Ghats profile line and stop markers.
- **Pale Gold Foil** (gold-300): gold as type and line on green grounds: the masthead, folio and section numerals, the search phrase underlines, the primary button's hover fill, and the default focus outline.

### Tertiary
- **Laterite** (laterite): Kerala red earth, used only for state: today's date in the calendar and validation messages in the guest picker. Never decorative.

### Neutral
- **Uncoated Ivory** (ivory): the reading-spread ground (Selection, Sea to the Ghats, Letters, the search pickers and sheet) and the primary text colour on green grounds.
- **Ivory Shade** (ivory-2): the photo-frame fill behind loading images on ivory, the highlighted option in the where list, and the middle of a calendar range.
- **Earth** (earth): secondary text on ivory grounds: intros, captions, credits, hints, weekday labels. On green grounds secondary text is ivory at 70–85% opacity instead.

### Named Rules
**The Four Foils Rule.** Gold appears in four roles only: the masthead (on the cover and in its condensed top-bar, menu, and colophon forms); folio and section numerals, including package nights and host step numbers; the field underlines of the search phrases plus the Sea to the Ghats profile line and markers; and the primary button. Labels and links use ivory or earth.

**The Whole Spread Rule.** A section takes one ground edge to edge: canopy or forest on green spreads, ivory on reading spreads. Content never sits in a card of one ground floating on another; dividers are hairline rules in the text colour at 10–25% opacity.

**The Focus Follows Ground Rule.** Every element sets its outline colour from `--focus` (pale gold by default), so colour transitions never start from currentColor. Ivory grounds switch `--focus` to canopy. The visible ring is a 2px solid outline offset 3px.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Brygada 1918 (with Noto Serif Malayalam, Georgia, serif)
**Body Font:** Anek Latin with its width axis (with Anek Malayalam, system-ui, sans-serif)
**Label Font:** Anek Latin at width 80, the narrow caption cut

**Character:** A bookish, slightly calligraphic text serif set large for headlines, over a humanist sans that narrows for captions like a magazine's photo credits. The pairing is the user's pinned choice.

### Scale
Every size sits on one ladder. Steps are 1px apart up to 18px, 2px apart up to 32px, 4px apart up to 48px, and 8px apart above that, so neighbouring steps up to 72px differ by roughly 6 to 17 percent. Twenty-one fixed sizes (13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 20, 22, 24, 26, 28, 30, 32, 36, 40, 44, 48, 56, 64, 72 and 96px) and four fluid ranges cover everything the site renders. Brygada covers 18px and up, plus run-ins at body size; Anek covers 13 to 20px.

Phone means below 768px, tablet 768 to 1023px, desktop 1024px and up. Every role holds its size or steps up as the screen widens; none steps down. Item titles use five neighbouring steps (22 to 30px), each fitted to its section's layout; a new list takes the nearest existing step.

### Named Rules
**The Scale-Only Hierarchy Rule.** Hierarchy comes from size and face, never weight: every heading is Brygada at 400 with -0.01em tracking (the masthead -0.03em). The one heavier weight is the semibold total row of a price breakdown. No uppercase and no positive letter-spacing.

**The One Ladder Rule.** A new size lands on the ladder above and gets a row in the scale table before it ships. Size changes happen at 768px and 1024px only, plus the four fluid ranges.

**The Caption Wins Rule.** `.caption` sits outside Tailwind's layers, so its size and leading beat any text-size or leading utility on the same element. To size a caption differently, give the element another role; a utility class on a caption does nothing.

**The Malayalam Room Rule.** Under `lang="ml"`, every Brygada line opens to a line-height of 1.3, the masthead and cover line included, tracking returns to 0, and the masthead and cover line step down to `display-ml` and `cover-line-ml`. In headings, paragraphs, and list items a long Malayalam word may break inside itself when nothing else fits a 320px column (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`); buttons and links keep their words whole. Malayalam text falls back to Noto Serif Malayalam and Anek Malayalam inside the same stacks; neither face is preloaded.

## Layout

Content sits in a centred container of 1440px with side gutters of 16px on phones, 24px from 768px, and 48px from 1024px. On large screens every section uses a 12-column grid with 48px gaps: headings usually span 6 columns with the intro set from column 8, and photographs are placed asymmetrically (a lead plate over 8 columns, then a staggered three at different heights and aspect ratios). Sections pad 96px vertically on phones, 128px from 768px, and 160px from 1024px.

Phone layouts are recomposed, not compressed: the search sentence breaks one phrase per line, the staggered photographs become compact rows with a small portrait thumbnail, the contents page becomes tabs, and the Sea to the Ghats section turns vertical with the photo held sticky. Tap targets are at least 44px. Anchors scroll with 80px of top padding for the fixed bar.

**The One Grid Break Rule.** Sea to the Ghats is the only element that leaves the 12-column grid: its cross-section bleeds to both viewport edges. Nothing else bleeds, apart from the cover photograph that fills the first viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Depth comes from changing ground (canopy, forest, ivory), from photographs, and from hairline rules. The condensed top bar separates itself from the page with a 1px ivory rule at 12% opacity, drawn as a shadow so it does not change the bar's height.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Picker lift** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 60px -12px rgb(15 33 25 / 0.55)`): the inline where/when/who panel that opens below the search sentence on top of the cover photograph. It is the only shadow in the build.

**The Ink-and-Stock Rule.** Surfaces are printed, not lifted: no shadows on cards, photographs, buttons, or sections. A panel that floats over a photograph is the one exception.

## Shapes

Every corner is square (0px): buttons, inputs, pickers, sheets, photo frames, tags, and calendar day buttons. Photographs crop to fixed ratios (4:5, 1:1, 3:2, 4:3, 16:9) and are clipped by their frame. The only non-rectangular forms are the Sea to the Ghats land profile and its stop markers, small squares rotated 45 degrees with a 2px ivory border.

**The Square Corner Rule.** No radius anywhere, including third-party widgets; the calendar's day buttons are overridden to 0.

## Components

### Buttons
Few and plain: one gold primary, one outlined secondary, and text links for everything else.
- **Shape:** square (0px).
- **Primary:** gold fill, canopy text, 18px body text, 56px tall with 32px side padding (48px tall and 24px side padding inside a package). Full width on phones. A trailing arrow nudges 4px right on hover.
- **Hover / Focus:** fill shifts to pale gold over the default colour transition; focus is the system ring.
- **Secondary:** transparent with a canopy border at 40% opacity, 44px tall, 20px side padding, 15px text. On hover the border goes full canopy over a 5% canopy wash. A pressed toggle (such as "I'm flexible") fills solid canopy with ivory text.
- **Icon buttons:** 44–48px squares with the same outline (steppers, letter arrows) or bare (close).

### Links
- **Drawn underline:** navigation, folios, and summaries draw a 1px current-colour underline from the left on hover and focus (0.45s, ease-out-quart).
- **In-text:** quiet resting underline at 40% current colour, 0.3em offset, that gives way to the drawn line on hover.

### Search sentence (signature)
The cover's working search is a sentence ("Stay in anywhere in Kerala, add dates, for two adults.") whose phrases are buttons. Each phrase carries a pale gold underline at 0.06em, thickening to 0.1em on hover and 0.12em when its step is current; on ivory the underline is forest. Punctuation sits inside the phrase so it never wraps away. On desktop the pickers open below the sentence as an ivory panel; on phones the sentence opens a full-screen ivory sheet with the sentence rebuilding live at the top and a sticky "Find stays" bar. Phrase changes animate with view transitions (0.45s).

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** text fields are underline-only: transparent, a 2px canopy rule at 30% opacity, title-size Brygada text, earth placeholder. Selects are a 1px canopy outline at 40% on ivory.
- **Focus:** the underline goes full canopy; the outline ring is suppressed only on this field because the rule itself carries focus.
- **Error:** laterite caption text below the field group.
- **Steppers:** label in title type with an earth hint, a 44px outlined minus and plus around a tabular Brygada count; disabled at 35% opacity.

### Navigation
- **Top bar:** 64px, transparent over the cover with the issue line in caption type; once the cover leaves, it condenses to a forest bar showing the masthead in pale gold and a compact outlined search summary. Links are 15px ivory with drawn underlines. Below 1024px a Menu button opens a full-screen forest dialog with the contents.
- **Folio strip:** a 64px forest band along the bottom of the cover listing numbered sections, the numerals in pale gold Brygada.

### Figures
**The Credited Photograph Rule.** A photograph is never shown without a caption and credit beneath it, in label type (earth on ivory, ivory at 70–75% on green): what it shows, a placeholder note while stock is used, and the photographer.

Frames fill with ivory-2 or canopy while loading. Where hover exists, photos inside a hovered or focused item scale to 1.03 over 0.6s inside their frame.

### Placeholder tag
**The Visible Placeholder Rule.** Every sample stay, package, letter, field note, and amount carries an inline "Placeholder" tag: label type at 14px, a 1px current-colour border at 45% opacity, 1px by 6px padding. Styling never hides it.

### Price details
**The Full Price Rule.** Every price shows "from ₹ — per night" (Brygada for the amount, tabular figures) with a "Price details" disclosure listing base rate, GST, service fee, and a semibold total, ruled with hairlines. The note follows the dates in the search; packages compute for their own night count and take only a start date from the search. Without dates it asks for them.

### Ledger rows
Packages and host steps are ruled lists, not cards: a large pale gold numeral (nights, step number) in a fixed left column, the title in Brygada, details in body small, and hairline rules between rows. A package row opens in place to its photograph, inclusions, price details, and a primary button.

### Reveal motion
Text reveals rise 12px and fade over 0.8s (ease-out-quart), staggered 60ms per item. Images uncover from the bottom by clip-path over 0.9s (ease-out-expo) while settling from scale 1.06. Each element reveals once. Because a target clipped to `inset(100%)` intersects with zero area, image reveals are observed through their parent. Content is visible without JavaScript and is forced visible if the observer has not started after 2.5s. Reduced motion removes reveals, parallax, zoom, and cross-fades.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** give each section one whole ground: canopy or forest for green spreads, ivory for reading spreads.
- **Do** keep gold to its four roles: masthead, numerals, search and profile lines, and the primary button.
- **Do** set every heading in Brygada 1918 at 400 and build hierarchy from size alone.
- **Do** keep every corner square (0px), including calendars and inputs.
- **Do** put a caption and credit under every photograph and a visible Placeholder tag on every sample.
- **Do** show a full price breakdown (base, GST, service fee, total) with every price.
- **Do** set `--focus` to canopy on any new ivory ground, using the existing on-ivory treatment.
- **Do** recompose phone layouts rather than shrinking desktop ones, with 44px minimum targets.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use gold for labels, links, tabs, or active states; use ivory or earth.
- **Don't** put content in rounded cards, floating white pills, or shadowed tiles.
- **Don't** add shadows beyond the picker panel that floats over the cover photograph.
- **Don't** use uppercase, tracked caps, or bold headings.
- **Don't** break the 12-column grid anywhere except Sea to the Ghats.
- **Don't** use laterite decoratively; it marks today's date and validation only.
- **Don't** show a fee or tax for the first time at checkout, or a star rating or verified badge anywhere.
